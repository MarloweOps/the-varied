Director's Intent

The song is about a room as much as it's about a relationship.

Twenty-four years. The smoke is a character. The objects are evidence. Caitlin isn't performing a story — she's the only witness to it. Two concepts. One vision.

04 / 11
Witness
  • Concept Caitlin walks through real LA locations — present-day. In each space, the couple appears as memory: overexposed, slightly soft, untouchable. When she leaves, they disappear. They can't follow her out.
  • Camera Steadicam for Caitlin. Handheld for memory. Shoot through things — bars, doorframes, glass. Never unobstructed.
  • Location Wide spaces. 40+ feet of depth. Craftsman or Spanish revival. The camera lives in the threshold, not the room.
  • Grade Two separate LUTs — no blending. Present: warm, saturated, golden. Memory: cool, desaturated, two stops blown.
  • Format Sony FX3 · Vintage Zeiss · 1.85:1

07 / 11
Objects
  • Concept One stage. Total darkness. Objects lit under a single warm source — screen left. Caitlin sings from a clean bright room, holding artifacts like she's trying to figure out how much each one weighs.
  • Lighting Dutch Golden Age. One source. Oblique shadows. Every shadow tells you something about the shape of the object casting it. The darkness is as designed as the light.
  • The Ashtray Overhead. Smoke rising. Hold until the smoke is the whole frame. Night-swimming laps in the ashtray. This is the title shot.
  • Stage 40–50 ft. The set is physically stripped between sequences. The room gets smaller. The pullback at the end reveals how much space became nothing.
  • Format ARRI Mini LF · Vintage Anamorphic · 2.39:1

Key Frames · Objects

The Evidence

01
The Ring

CZ ring on white cloth under a single practical. Lit like it costs everything and nothing. The shadow it casts is longer than it should be.

02
Pill Bottles

100mm, f/2.0. Labels just past readable — you feel the weight without reading the diagnosis. Amber glass in warm light.

03
The Hole

Drywall at eye level. Caitlin's hand enters frame from below, touches the edge. Then pulls back. Camera holds on the wall.

04
The Ashtray

Overhead. Smoke rising in available light. Hold until the smoke fills the frame. This is the title. This is the song.

Production

Two Approaches

Witness
Location · Lower Budget
  • Camera Sony FX3 · Vintage Zeiss or Leica glass
  • Format 1.85:1 · Two visual registers
  • Schedule 2 days · Golden hour shoots both days
  • Location Real LA · 40+ ft depth minimum · Craftsman or loft
  • Grade Split LUT · Present warm · Memory blown
  • Crew Small, tight · Intimacy is the point
Objects
Stage · Higher Budget
  • Camera ARRI Mini LF · Vintage Anamorphic glass
  • Format 2.39:1 · Stage reveal on final pullback
  • Schedule Pre-light + 2 days · Day 1 objects · Day 2 Caitlin
  • Stage 40–50 ft · Dutch lighting rig · Total control
  • Grade Full DI · Forensic gold · ARRI color science
  • Art Dept Full prop budget · Set stripped between sequences
